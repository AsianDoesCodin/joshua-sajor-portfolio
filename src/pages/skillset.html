Skillset

A side-by-side demo that shows business owners why an AI assistant needs their own skills, not a generic prompt.

01Problem

Generic AI chatbots guess at prices, hours and policies, and get them wrong in front of customers. Owners can't see the difference until it costs them a job.

02Solution

The same customer message goes to two assistants at once: one generic, one running on the business's own skills (price sheet, schedule, service area, voice and policies). Visitors pick one of three businesses or type their own question.

03Result

In one click, a vague guess sits next to a correct, on-brand answer with real prices and next steps. Every answer is a pre-written script and labelled that way, so the demo never overclaims.
