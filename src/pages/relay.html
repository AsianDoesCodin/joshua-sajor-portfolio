RELAY

A back-office automation hub for a plumbing and HVAC company, where every flow is visible, testable and accounted for.

01Problem

Service businesses lose jobs to missed calls and lose hours chasing invoices, reviews and reminders by hand. Automations that run out of sight make owners nervous.

02Solution

A console in black and electric lime. Six automations are drawn as node graphs (web-lead follow-up, missed-call text-back, invoices and review requests, overdue reminders, dispatch texts), each with test runs, streaming step logs and an output preview.

03Result

Owners see exactly what ran, when, and what it saved. On the sample business: 1,600+ tasks automated in a month, lead response down to 38 seconds and over $7,000 recovered by reminders, from simulated runs that say so on screen.

Stack

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • SVG node graphs
  • Simulated run engine
